The plugin code, themes' CSS and original scenery are distributed under [MIT](LICENSE).

## Original Piora scenery

Sage Paper, Moss Forest, Aurora Glass, Midnight Nebula, Auspicious Clouds, Crimson Moon, After School Sky, and Neon Rain are original Piora backgrounds. Piora's contributors generated them independently from text prompts. This bundle preserves the WebP files and adds theme palettes and illustrative card previews. See the [Piora scenery notice](https://github.com/kexijiang/pi-desktop/blob/3d931edd4f197370e43ddb3ef3108104ec22f819/public/themes/dream-backgrounds/README.md).

## Character artwork

- Original character designs by [ZipZipPipe（胖次狐）](https://space.bilibili.com/4168597).
